Maël Bompais

Postdoctoral Research Associate
University of Nottingham, United Kingdom

My research focuses on quantum trajectories, the stochastic evolution of quantum systems conditioned on measurement outcomes. I am particularly interested in their long-time behaviour, stability and feedback control, and more broadly in how measurement can be used to understand and manipulate quantum systems.

Email: mael.bompais@nottingham.ac.uk
